5. Target test

Pick one target of the catalog, set a value and check that the VR-09 took it. For a parameter address, "Send and check" reads the address before and after the write, so the result does not depend on hearing a change; for a CC it compares the VR-09's state before and after, as section 5 does. Click a row of the table to select its target. Results are kept in library/target-tests.json. Stop the bridge first.

Writes each one a different value, reads it back, and puts the original back.

6. Scenes and single tones

A single tone is one tone of the VR-09's tone list, played alone on the upper synth or the upper piano, with all its sound-shaping values at their neutral setting. Choose a tone category, a mapping (what each Touché axis controls), and click Load on a tone. Then hold a note, move each axis, and click "works" or "no effect" on that axis in the monitor on the right. The answers are kept in library/target-tests.json.

A scene is a registration of the library paired with a Touché mapping. "Load" on a row writes the registration to the VR-09, loads the mapping into section 7 and starts the bridge, so you can play and feel the Touché at once. The registration is written as live values, about half a second of messages; pressing its Registration button on the keyboard's panel puts the keyboard's own copy back. Changing a row's mapping saves the pairing in the catalog, as the Pairings tab of the library page does.

In standalone mode the same buttons also change the Touché's own preset (the LED colour is its bank), which can change the CCs it sends.

7. Remap preset

Each of the four axes can drive several VR-09 targets at once, each over its own range, window of the axis travel and curve. A range may descend (low above high) to invert. Targets are grouped by how much we trust them: "verified" were written alone on the unit, "recalled" changed when a dump was recalled, "documented" are only in Roland's document.
