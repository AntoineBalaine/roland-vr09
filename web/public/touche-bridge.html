5. CC out test

Because the VR-09 documents some CCs but none was ever tested live, this panel sends one CC and, with "Send and compare", reads the VR-09's state before and after, so the result does not depend on hearing a change. The channels are those of MIDI IN Mode 2.

6. Remap preset

Each of the four axes can drive several VR-09 targets at once, each over its own range, window of the axis travel and curve. A range may descend (low above high) to invert. Targets are grouped by how much we trust them: "verified" were written alone on the unit, "recalled" changed when a dump was recalled, "documented" are only in Roland's document.
